**Status:** In progress  
**Tools:** Wazuh, VirtualBox, Kali Linux, Metasploitable 2

A home lab designed to practice SIEM deployment, virtual network configuration, controlled reconnaissance, and security-event investigation. I have installed Wazuh in VirtualBox and am working through connectivity and network configuration before beginning the monitoring exercises.

**Planned deliverables**
- Lab architecture and isolated network design.
- Setup notes and connectivity troubleshooting.
- Controlled reconnaissance against the lab target.
- Review of available logs and Wazuh alerts.
- Investigation notes, screenshots, and remediation recommendations.

## 🔎 Vulnerability Assessment & Threat Intelligence

**Status:** Learning project / report in development  
**Tools:** Nmap, vulnerability references, CVE/CVSS concepts, MITRE ATT&CK

A project focused on understanding how to enumerate exposed services, research relevant vulnerability information, assess the significance of findings, and write practical remediation recommendations.

**Planned deliverables**
- Scope and authorized test environment.
- Sanitized scan output and service observations.
- Research notes for relevant CVEs, where applicable.
- Risk discussion and remediation guidance.
- Limitations and lessons learned.

No vulnerability will be described as confirmed unless the evidence supports that conclusion.
